## Why this keeps happening, and what actually fixes it

A dispute clears one file. The next release is a new hash, and an unsigned NSIS bundle will
often draw a fresh machine-learning flag, so this is a recurring cost rather than a one-off.

Code signing is the durable fix. In rough order of cost:

- **Azure Trusted Signing**, around USD 10 per month. By far the cheapest route to a signed
  binary, but it requires a legal entity in the US or Canada that is three or more years old.
- **OV certificate**, roughly USD 200-400 per year. Signs the binary, but SmartScreen
  reputation still starts at zero and builds with download volume.
- **EV certificate**, roughly USD 400-700 per year plus a hardware token. Carries SmartScreen
  reputation immediately.

Until one of those is in place, expect to repeat this per release, and keep the site and README
honest that the builds are unsigned.
